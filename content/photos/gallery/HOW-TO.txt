GALLERY
Every folder here is one album. Put the year in the folder name, first if you can:
    2026 - Cerebro finals
    2025 - Delphique 29th edition
The year puts the album under that year on the Gallery page.

Several albums in one year? Add the month to keep them in order (newest first):
    2026-12 - Cerebro finals
    2026-11 - Opening day

Photo names inside an album don't matter (IMG_4521.jpg is fine), and iPhone photos work.
Pick your best 30-50 photos per album.

To add an album: make a folder with that name on your laptop, put the photos in,
then Add file > Upload files and drag the whole folder in.
To delete an album: open its folder > ... menu > Delete directory.
