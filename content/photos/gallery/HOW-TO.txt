GALLERY
The Gallery page shows every photo in this folder in one grid. Nothing else is shown:
no folder names, no years, no filters. Tap a photo to see it full size.

Folders are optional and only keep things tidy. With the year first in a folder's name,
newer photos come first on the page:
    2026 - Delphique
    2025 - Delphique 29th edition

Photo names don't matter (IMG_4521.jpg is fine), and iPhone photos work.
Pick your best photos: the gallery is for highlights.

To add photos: open a folder > Add file > Upload files, and drag them in.
To add a folder: make it on your laptop with its name, put the photos in, then drag the whole folder in.
To delete photos: open the photo or folder > ... menu > Delete file / Delete directory.
