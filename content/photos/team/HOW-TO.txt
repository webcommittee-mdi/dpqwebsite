THE PHOTO (so everyone's card matches)
    Shape:       upright, 4:5  (e.g. 1080 x 1350 px; at least 800 x 1000 px)
    Framing:     head and shoulders, face in the middle, a little space above the head,
                 eyes about a third of the way down. The face (chin to top of the head)
                 takes up about a third of the photo's height.
    Background:  the red brick wall the rest of the team used (stand an arm's length in
                 front of it, so the wall fills everything behind you). Everyone in a
                 group should have the same kind of background.
    Look:        formals, facing the light (no window or sun behind you), no filters,
                 no portrait-mode blur.
    File:        JPG straight from the phone camera, not sent through WhatsApp.
A photo in another shape still works: the website crops it from the top to 4:5.
The homepage strip shows everyone except the Website Team, who are on the Team page.
