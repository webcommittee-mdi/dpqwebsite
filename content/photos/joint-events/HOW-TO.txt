JOINT EVENT POSTERS (optional)
Name each poster exactly like the event in joint-events.txt:
    event = Alumni Talk | Alumni Committee | 14 November 2026 |
    ->  Alumni Talk.jpg

The Instagram poster works as it is (4:5 or square). Posters are never cropped.
Events without a poster show a simple tile with the club's name instead.

Old posters can stay: once an event's date has passed it disappears from the
website by itself. Delete them now and then to keep this folder tidy.
