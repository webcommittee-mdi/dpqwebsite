JOINT EVENT AND PANEL DISCUSSION POSTERS (optional)
Name each poster exactly like the event in joint-events.txt:
    event = Alumni Talk | Alumni Committee
    ->  Alumni Talk.jpg
    event = The Future of HR Policy Making | Panel Discussion
    ->  The Future of HR Policy Making.jpg

Any poster shape works: a phone story (tall), an Instagram post (4:5) or a square.
Posters are never cut off. All the cards take the shape most of the posters have.
Events without a poster show a tile with the event's name instead.

Old posters can stay: once an event's date has passed it disappears from the
website by itself. Delete them now and then to keep this folder tidy.
