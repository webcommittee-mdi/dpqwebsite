EVENT CARD PHOTOS
One photo per event, named after the event's short ID in [brackets] in events.txt:
    cerebro.jpg   vincenza.jpg   panel-discussion.jpg   mun.jpg

Wide (landscape) photos work best: the card is wide and shows the middle of the photo.
Keep people and action near the centre (an upright poster is cut off at the top and bottom).
The SAMPLE photo for an event hides itself as soon as you upload a real one.

To change a real photo later, delete the old one and upload the new one.
