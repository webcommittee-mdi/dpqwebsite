HOMEPAGE SLIDESHOW
Photos here rotate in the big image on the homepage.

Name them with a number (sets the order) and a caption:
    01 - Opening ceremony.jpg
    02 - Keynote.jpg
The caption appears beside the slideshow dots. 3 to 5 wide photos work best.

Keep the main subject in the CENTRE: on laptops only the middle and right of the
photo show; on phones the centre sits behind the text. Dark, atmospheric shots look best.

Any photo straight from a phone or camera works (iPhone photos too).
The SAMPLE photos hide themselves as soon as you add one real photo.
