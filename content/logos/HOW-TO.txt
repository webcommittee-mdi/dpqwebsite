LOGOS
When a logo is added here, it replaces the plain text on the website.

    A file with "delphique" in its name (e.g. delphique.png)  -> top bar and footer.
        Use a version made for dark backgrounds (white or light text).
    A file with "mdi" in its name (e.g. mdi.png)  -> footer and About page.

SVG or a transparent PNG looks best.
Subfolders: events/ for event logos, partners/ for sponsor logos.
